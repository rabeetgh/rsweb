Research
Electronic structure theory · Density functional theory · Computational materials
Computational Quantum Physics and Few-Body Quantum Systems
Numerical and theoretical investigation of quantum systems, including confined quantum systems, electron correlation, quantum dots, and few-body problems.
Computational Materials Science and Nanostructures
Theoretical investigation of the electronic, structural, and physical properties of materials and nanoscale systems using computational methods.
Density Functional Theory and Electronic Structure Methods
Development, implementation, and assessment of exchange-correlation functionals, Hartree–Fock method, and other electronic structure approaches for atomic and molecular systems.
Numerical Methods and Scientific Computing
Development of computational algorithms and high-precision numerical techniques, including finite-element methods, adaptive meshing, eigenvalue solvers, numerical integration, and high-performance scientific computing.
Machine Learning for Quantum Physics and Electronic Structure
Machine-learning-assisted exchange-correlation functionals, neural-network representations of quantum states and densities, surrogate models for electronic structure calculations, physics-informed machine learning, and data-driven approaches to quantum many-body problems.
